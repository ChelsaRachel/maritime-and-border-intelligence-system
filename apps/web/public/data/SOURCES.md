# MBIS frontend fixture provenance

All operational records in this folder are static, synthetic demonstration data. They are designed to look credible but do not describe current real-world military, border, vessel, or aircraft activity.

Reference sources used before fixture creation:

- International Maritime Organization — AIS identity, position, course, speed, and navigational-status field model: https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/safety/pages/ais.aspx
- Global Fishing Watch — apparent fishing effort and AIS activity methodology: https://globalfishingwatch.org/dataset-and-code-fishing-effort/
- Global Fishing Watch — vessel identity and registry reconciliation patterns: https://globalfishingwatch.org/datasets-and-code-vessel-identity/
- BMKG Maritime — Indonesian maritime-area naming and wave-height classification: https://maritim.bmkg.go.id/
- OpenSky Network — aircraft metadata and state-vector dataset concepts: https://opensky-network.org/data/scientific
- BNPP — official PLBN names and service context: https://bnpp.go.id/plbn
- Directorate General of Sea Transportation — Indonesian main-port references: https://hubla.dephub.go.id/home/post/read/5603/kemenhub-luncurkan-aplikasi-inaportnet-2-0-dan-do-online-di-lima-pelabuhan

No live feed, provider credential, personal data, or restricted registry is embedded in these files.
